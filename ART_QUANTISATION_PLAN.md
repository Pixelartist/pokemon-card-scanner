# Quantising the German card art

An idea, what it was measured to save, and why nothing has been applied to it
yet. The art itself lives in `data/german_cards/images/` (gitignored); this file
only records the numbers and the decision.

## The problem

`validate()` in `scripts/fetch_german_art.py` decodes each download and saves it
as a 24-bit PNG, which is the right format for a lossless complaint but a poor
one for photographic card art. A 25 KB PokéWiki JPEG becomes a 199 KB PNG, and
a 600×825 scan lands around 300–500 KB.

Measured over the installed store on 2026-11-23:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Files | 17,386 `de_*.png` |
| Total | 7.17 GiB |
| Mean / median / p90 | 433 KB / 306 KB / 641 KB |

| Width bucket | Files | Size | Share | Mean |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| < 300 px | 266 | 62 MB | 0.8% | 226 KB |
| 300–499 | 563 | 320 MB | 4.2% | 556 KB |
| 500–799 | 15,884 | 5,650 MB | 73.3% | 347 KB |
| 800+ | 673 | 1,681 MB | 21.8% | 2,439 KB |

58% of that (4.17 GiB, the 14,787 reconstructed entries) has no source URL in
`data/german_art_sources.json`, so any lossy re-encode of those files cannot be
undone by re-fetching.

## What was measured

Six files sampled per bucket, re-encoded from the installed art on 2026-11-23:

| Bucket | current | `optimize=True` | palette 256 | palette 128 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| < 300 | 208 KB | 211 KB (0%) | 65 KB (−69%) | 57 KB |
| 300–499 | 580 KB | 616 KB (+6%) | 161 KB (−72%) | 138 KB |
| 500–799 | 512 KB | 512 KB (0%) | 165 KB (−68%) | 158 KB |
| 800+ | 2,721 KB | 1,778 KB (−2%) | 611 KB (−78%) | 503 KB |

- **`optimize=True` is not the fix.** It buys 0% to −2% on photographic art and
  costs 2–3.5× the CPU (105 ms → 352 ms at 800+). The comment already in
  `validate()` about it doubling CPU is right; the payoff it implies is not
  there.
- **Colour quantisation is.** A 256-colour palette PNG saves 68–78% and is
  *faster* to write than the current encode (7–31 ms vs 10–105 ms), because a
  palette compresses trivially.
- **Quality:** PSNR 30–39 dB, worst-case per-pixel error 50–59/255.
- **Matching:** fingerprint drift between original and quantised art is
  ≤ 0.0007 (0.0020 at 128 colours), against `MATCH_DIST 0.16` and
  `WEAK_DIST 0.30` — roughly 230× inside the match threshold, so the scanner is
  unaffected.
- **Store-wide estimate:** 7.17 GiB → ~2.3 GiB, about 68% or 4.9 GiB.

## The proof sheet

`data/quantisation-proof.png` (generated by `data/proof_sheet.py`) puts the
original beside the 256-colour version for four cards, full size and at a 3×
nearest-neighbour crop of a busy region:

| Card | Source | Size | Original → 256 | PSNR |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `de_dp7-SH2` | pokewiki, 250×349 | 177 KB → 55 KB | −69% | 31.6 dB |
| `de_dp1-1` | pokewiki, 465×650 | 581 KB → 167 KB | −71% | 31.7 dB |
| `de_30th-001` | rebuilt, 600×825 | 298 KB → 137 KB | −54% | 40.2 dB |
| `de_dp1-10` | bisafans, 842×1200 | 1,451 KB → 436 KB | −70% | 32.6 dB |

At real size the pairs are indistinguishable and German text stays legible. The
only place banding is visible is the 842 px Bisafans photo, where the dark
purple space background posterises slightly at 3× magnification; flat cartoon
art quantises essentially perfectly (40 dB).

## Options

1. **Change `validate()` to save a 256-colour palette PNG.** Three or four lines
   and free at runtime, but it only affects *new* installs. The repair path
   writes one card at a time — the 228 low-res German cards, if every one were
   repaired, would save about 33 MB. Worth doing only alongside bulk fetching.
2. **Sweep the existing store.** The real prize: ~4.9 GiB. 15–20 minutes of CPU
   plus I/O over the data share. Two sub-decisions:
   - the 3.01 GiB of `recorded` art can be re-fetched if a quantised version
     disappoints, so that half is reversible;
   - the 4.17 GiB of reconstructed art has no URL, so quantising it is final.
3. **Leave it alone.** 7.17 GiB on a NAS volume that already holds the art is
   not urgent, and 256 colours on the largest photographic scans is the one
   place where the loss is visible.

## Recommendation

Do not change the default yet. If disk becomes a problem, run the sweep over the
`recorded` files first (reversible, ~2 GiB) and decide on the reconstructed half
separately — and only after looking at the proof sheet on a real screen, since
the banding judgement is visual, not something PSNR settles.

## Status

- Nothing has been changed in `scripts/fetch_german_art.py`; `validate()` still
  writes 24-bit PNG and the store is untouched (manifest md5
  `a0f4ffbf…`, 17,348 entries, 14,787 reconstructed).
- The two probing scripts used for the numbers were throwaway and have been
  deleted; `data/proof_sheet.py` is kept so the sheet can be regenerated with a
  different set of cards.
- Both `data/quantisation-proof.png` and `data/proof_sheet.py` are in the
  gitignored `data/` directory: they are evidence, not shipped code.
